Vital browser extension privacy policy

Effective 2026-09-15 · Product: Chrome extension “Vital”

This policy describes how the Vital extension handles data. The extension is provided by Vital (https://vital.aimo.plus). It saves web content into your own Vital account when you choose to save.

What we collect

Only when you click the toolbar, use the shortcut, or pick a context-menu item:

  • The page URL, title, extracted article text, and any text you selected
  • Images you choose to save, and PDF / audio / video files you choose to save
  • The email used to sign in, plus access and refresh tokens stored in Chrome storage

We do not record browsing history for pages you did not save, and we do not scan other tabs.

How we use it

Only to write items into your Vital read-later inbox or tasks, and to show recent saves in the extension. Not for advertising. Not sold. Not given to third parties unrelated to operating Vital.

Where it is stored

  • Account and items: Vital servers (https://vital.aimo.plus)
  • Saved images and files: object storage used by Vital (https://s3.aimo.plus)
  • Auth tokens: on-device chrome.storage (access token in session storage, refresh token in local storage)

Retention and deletion

Items and files stay with your Vital account until you delete them or close the account. Uninstalling the extension clears on-device tokens and does not automatically delete content already saved on the server.

Permissions

storage, activeTab, scripting, contextMenus, offscreen, and host permissions for Vital domains are used only to save into Vital. The extension does not load remotely hosted scripts.

Contact

Privacy requests: through the account system at https://vital.aimo.plus.
